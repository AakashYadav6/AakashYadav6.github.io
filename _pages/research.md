## Research Work

### [Design and Development of Amphibious Unmanned Aerial Vehicle](https://docs.google.com/document/d/135Z5TY74YDIhVaIr5FEqVyAM0nOg1VQJ/edit?usp=sharing&ouid=105327663954184394090&rtpof=true&sd=true)

- Designed an **Amphibious UAV (A-UAV)** with a **coaxial rotor system**, reducing power consumption by **15%** while improving thrust and transition behavior.
- Conducted **structural, stability, and aerodynamic analysis** using **SolidWorks**, **ANSYS**, and **SimScale**.
- Led **material and electronic component selection**, including thrust, weight, center of mass, and center of buoyancy analysis.
- Integrated **ESCs, Brushless DC Motors, and Raspberry Pi** for PWM-based propulsion control.
