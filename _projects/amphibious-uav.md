- Designed an **Amphibious UAV (A-UAV)** with a **coaxial rotor system** and tilt-rotor mechanism.
- Reduced power consumption by **15%** while improving thrust generation and transition dynamics.
- Performed **SolidWorks**, **ANSYS**, and **SimScale** based structural and aerodynamic evaluation.
- Integrated **ESCs**, **Brushless DC Motors**, and **Raspberry Pi** for propulsion control.
